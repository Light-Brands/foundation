# The Ninja framework

Your business is a set of departments, and each department is a set of jobs. The Ninja framework hands those jobs to AI agents, skills and routines, as deep as you choose, all steered from one command center, until the whole business runs as one system. Every entry in this dictionary answers two questions: which job it takes off your plate, and what you still decide.

Start with [the guide](guide.md). It is short, and it shows you how to ask.

Dictionary version 0.3.0.

## How to ask for anything

Say what you want to your AI assistant, in plain words. Seven verbs cover everything:

- **Install** adds something new. "Install the Monday brief."
- **Modify** changes something you have, to fit how you work. "Modify my Monday brief so it arrives on Tuesdays."
- **Upgrade** moves something you have to a newer version or a deeper tier. "Upgrade the Monday brief to Complete."
- **Check** tells you where your business stands and what to do next. "Check my system."
- **Remove** takes something out cleanly, after saving its data. "Remove the Monday brief."
- **Learn** explains how anything works, at any depth. "Learn how routines work."
- **Weave**, coming, will connect what you have installed into one system. "Weave my sales and delivery together." Today it checks what you have and explains what weaving them will do.

Every request runs the same way: a few questions, one plan you approve, then the crew builds, reviews, tests and proves it. Nothing is written until you approve the plan. The [runner](runner/run.md) is the full set of steps.

**Autopilot first, and by number.** Every run starts by asking what can run on its own: it connects the tools you already use, schedules what can be scheduled, and leaves you only the decisions. Each job also has a number, shown on the command center's page and listed in [the directory](foundation/directory.md), so you can ask "follow" with a job's name or number as well as "install the Monday brief". Your assistant looks in [the library](library.md) first: this dictionary, then the Dojo Dictionary online, and builds from scratch only when no entry does the job.

## The path

1. **Prep.** Get your computer and your AI tools ready.
2. **Home.** Build your Ninja home: one folder that holds your business, your roles and their skills.
3. **Foundation.** Deploy your Foundation, the command center, on top of your home. Your home moves in as it is.
4. **Build.** Install, shape and weave the jobs of your business, one at a time.

"Install the Ninja framework into my environment" runs the first three steps in order, starting from wherever you are.
