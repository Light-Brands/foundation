# The guide

This is the one page you need to use the Ninja framework. The weekly calls teach the rest.

## How to ask

Talk to your AI assistant the way you would talk to a capable colleague. Name what you want done and the job it is for.

- **In Claude Code, inside your Foundation folder,** just say it. The dictionary is already inside your Foundation.
- **Anywhere else,** add "with the Ninja framework from dojo.lightbrands.ai" so your assistant knows where to look.

Seven sentences that work today:

1. "Install the Ninja framework into my environment."
2. "Install the Monday brief."
3. "Modify my Monday brief so it arrives on Tuesdays."
4. "Upgrade the Monday brief to Complete."
5. "Check my system."
6. "Remove the Monday brief."
7. "Learn how routines work."

And one that is coming: "Weave Sales and Delivery so a signed contract starts a project." Weaving arrives in a coming update. Today your assistant checks what you have and explains what the weave will do. Installs for the department jobs, such as Capture leads, arrive in coming updates too.

You never need the exact name. "Something that sums up my week" finds the Monday brief. If two entries fit, your assistant asks which one you mean.

You can also ask by number with the word follow. Every job has one, shown on its page in your command center and in [the directory](foundation/directory.md), so "follow Story and voice" works as well as "install". Your assistant looks the job up by its name or its number: it installs the entry when you do not have it yet, offers to upgrade or modify it when you do, and plans the job with you when the dictionary has no entry for it yet. Whatever you ask, your assistant starts with autopilot first: it asks which tools you use for the job today, connects them, puts what can run on its own on a schedule, and leaves you only the decisions. Before it builds anything, it looks in [the library](library.md) for an entry that already does the job.

## What happens next

Your assistant asks a few short questions, one at a time, and skips anything it already knows. Then it shows you one plan: every file it will create or change, and how it will prove the work. Nothing is written until you say yes to that plan. Then the crew builds it, reviews it, tests it and runs one real item through it. You decide anything that goes outside your folder, such as a preview, a test message, or going live.

Each run ends by telling you what changed and what to do next.

## Choose your depth

Every entry comes in three tiers. You pick one when you ask, and you can upgrade later.

- **Essentials:** the simplest version that does the job, working within the hour.
- **Complete:** the full version, with its automations.
- **Mastery:** the deepest version, with AI agents, reporting and loops that keep improving it.

If you are unsure, start with Essentials. Upgrading keeps your own changes, and asks you when you and the new version changed the same part.

## One thing at a time

You can ask for several things at once. Your assistant will recommend doing one at a time and offer the rest as next moves. One finished job is worth more than three half-built ones.

## The levels

Levels describe what the weekly calls teach. Choose your own level and your own depth. Nothing here is locked behind a level.

| Level | The weekly call shows you how to | You will mostly use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Use the Foundation and the system: deploy it, find your way around, ask with the seven verbs | the frameworks, this guide, a first module |
| 2 | Automate your work: build agents, skills and routines, install modules | modules, agents, skills, routines, connections |
| 3 | Weave it all into one complete system | weave, blueprints |

The weekly workshop is always Level 1, run live: a Foundation set up from the Dojo link.

## Momentum, with steering

This system is built to move fast. Every run ends on your next move, defaults are chosen so you only answer what matters, and "Check my system" always tells you where you stand and what is ready to go further. The speed is safe because of the steering: you approve every plan, every change is reviewed and tested, one real item is proven before anything counts as done, and nothing goes live without your yes in that moment. You set the direction, and the crew keeps it on course.

## When you want to know more

Ask to learn. "Learn how the Monday brief works." "Learn why the plan needs my approval." There is no course to finish first. The answer is there when you need it.

The full map of everything you can ask for is on [the front page](index.md).
