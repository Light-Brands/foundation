# Learn

Learn explains an entry, a verb or anything in the system, at any depth, at any moment. There is no course to finish first.

## What differs from the full runner

- **Read only.** No plan, no crew build stages, no run brief, and nothing written.
- **It stops after stage 3.** At stage 3 the [guide](../../crew/guide.md) answers, drawing on the entry's How it is done section, its bar and its tiers, and on [the Foundation primer](../../foundation/primer.md), [the Ninja home](../../foundation/ninja-home.md) and [the rules](../../foundation/rules.md).

## Stages

1. **Orient,** lightly: enough to know the business, what is installed and where the owner is on the path, so the answer uses their own business as the example.
2. **Questionnaire,** the first question only: what they want to understand. Then one question about depth when the request does not say it: "Would you like the short version, or how it works underneath?"
3. **Target.** The answer, at the depth asked, in plain words, with the business's own names in the examples.

## Questions

What to explain, and how deep. Nothing else.

## The close

Every answer ends on a next move: a deeper question worth asking, or the run that puts the idea to work, such as "When you are ready, say 'Install the Monday brief'."
